Who We Serve

For learners who are done with the default.

FLOE exists for the innovators, thinkers, creators, and system designers who live under the neurodiversity umbrella. We exist because the books stop too soon, the support systems end too early, and the world keeps measuring the wrong things.

Our Programs

Three pathways. One destination: yourself.

Every FLOE client enters the program that fits where they actually are, based on their discovery assessment.

W

WONDER

Rediscover · Reconnect · Reimagine

For learners who need to slow down and remember who they are before deciding where they're going. Open-ended, curiosity-driven, reflective.

"Before we go anywhere — let's remember who you are."
S

SOAR

Clarify · Build · Launch

For learners who have a sense of direction but keep hitting walls. Action-oriented, goal-focused, structured for momentum.

"You know where you want to go — let's build the runway."
SW

Soar with Wonder

Explore · Play · Grow · Wonder

For families with young learners (2–14) who want to build a culture of curiosity from the very beginning. Before the system defines them, we help families design a learning life that celebrates who their child already is — and nurtures who they're becoming.

"It's never too early to fall in love with learning."

How It Works

A full-year partnership, built around you.

  • 01
    Discovery Assessment Multi-perspective intake — learner, parent, and support. We map the whole ecosystem first.
  • 02
    Program Placement WONDER or SOAR — based on where you actually are, not where anyone thinks you should be.
  • 03
    Monthly 1:1 Sessions 12 sessions over the year. Real conversations, real progress.
  • 04
    Quarterly Benchmarks Progress on your terms — measuring what matters to you, not a standardized rubric.
  • 05
    Community & Mentor Network A cohort of 5–7 clients who grow together, plus a mentor network for ecosystem support.

What We Believe

We know this because we live it.

01 Neurodiversity is both a strength and a disability. We hold both truths without needing to resolve the tension between them.
02 Success looks different for every person because our brains and needs are different. We don't measure the wrong things.
03 Building bridges where gaps exist is the purpose of community. And community is built on strength, not deficit.
04 Everyone has the right to learn in the way that best suits them. The program bends like water.
05 The learner is always the center. Everything — including this program — orbits them.
